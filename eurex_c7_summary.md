## 1. Eurex C7 XML Report Overview
Eurex Clearing operates the **C7 Architecture**, utilizing structured XML reports delivered via the **Common Report Engine (CRE)**. Accurate integration requires distinguishing between fluid cash transactional logs and overall collateral pool evaluations.

### Key Reports Profile

| Report ID | Official Name | Primary Objective | Key Target Metrics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`CD710`** | Daily Cash Account Clearing Member | Bank-side and ledger cash reconciliation | Itemised cash transactions, banking inflows/outflows, `assetSource`. |
| **`CD020`** | Collateral Movement / Coverage | Real-time risk management and margin monitoring | Multi-asset pools (Cash + ISINs), `coverValue`, compliance surpluses/shortfalls. |

## 2. Structural Architecture & Core Metadata Tags

### `assetSource` (CD710 Specific)
A three-character alphanumeric identifier indicating which upstream source or business line triggered a cash transaction.
*   **`ALL`**: Shared transaction types applicable across multiple asset classes.
*   **`COL`**: Cash movements explicitly tied to Collateral Management, Clearing Fund changes, or Margin Calls.
*   **`SEC`**: Cash flows generated by Cash Markets (e.g., FWB, Repo markets, physical Eurex Deliveries).
*   **`ETD`**: Exchange Traded Derivatives transactions (Futures and Options).

### `clntSegMod` & `CASSO` (CD020 Specific)
*   **`clntSegMod` (Client Segregation Model)**: Defines the legal framework governing a specific collateral pool to guarantee asset protection under EMIR, LSOC, or standard regulations. Common values include **`ISA`** (Individual Segregated Account), **`OSA`** (Omnibus Segregated Account), **`GOSA`**, and **`NOSA`**.
*   **`CASSO` (UK CASS Segregation)**: Strings matching `CASS` or `CASSO` identify pools bound to the UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) **Client Money and Asset Rules**. Pledged assets within these pools are legally isolated under specific trust layouts and cannot offset house-level default risks.

## 3. Transaction Type (`trnTyp7`) Deep-Dive Dictionary

### CD710 Cash Account Transaction Types

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **206 / 207** | CASH COLLATERAL WDL / WDL1 | Credit (Inflow) | `COL` | Withdrawal of excess cash buffer back to the clearing member's bank. |

| **294** | CASH SETTLEMENT PAID | Debit (Outflow) | `ETD` | Realised cash settlement outflow at contract expiration. |
| **296** | CASH SETTLEMENT RCVD | Credit (Inflow) | `ETD` | Realised cash settlement inflow at contract expiration. |
| **324** | POS TRF WITH CSH DEB | Debit (Outflow) | `ETD` | Cash debit tied directly to an outgoing position transfer. |
| **325** | POS TRF WITH CSH CRD | Credit (Inflow) | `ETD` | Cash credit tied directly to an incoming position transfer. |

### CD020 Collateral Adjustments (Pool Metrics)
*   **`202` (Margin Call)**: External deposit adding cash/eligible assets into a pool from the clearing bank to fix a margin deficit.
*   **`207` (Cash Collateral WDL1)**: External withdrawal taking excess cash out of Eurex to decrease total pool `coverValue`.
*   **`213` (Cash Collateral Transfer Deposit)**: Internal pool inflow. Cash is moved into this pool directly from another internal corporate pool. No external banking wires are executed.
*   **`215` (Cash Collateral Transfer Withdrawal)**: Internal pool outflow. Cash buffer is moved out of this specific pool into an alternative internal pool.

## 4. Back-Office Reconciliation & Logic Rules

### The Core Cash Equation
To calculate your true **Ending Cash Balance** inside the CD710, use the sub-ledger equation:
$$	ext{Ending Cash Balance} = 	ext{Beginning Balance} + \sum(	ext{All Credits}) - \sum(	ext{All Debits})$$

### The Equity Computation Trap
> **Critical Rule:** You must never blindly sum all `trnTyp7` amounts together with the starting balance to calculate Total Trading Equity. Doing so causes severe double-counting due to the intersection of different ledgers.

To achieve clean financial accounting, segregate codes dynamically in your parser scripts:
1.  **Trading Realised P&L Ledger**: Group `235/236` (Variation Margin), `224/226` (Premium), and `294/296` (Cash Settlement) applying strict mathematical sign flags (+/-).
2.  **Collateral Funding Ledger**: Separate `200/202` and `206/207`. These adjust liquidity distributions between bank accounts and clearing pools, but they do not represent trading equity.
3.  **Non-Cash Securities Assets**: Multi-asset valuation (Bonds/Equities tracked via ISINs) must be computed from the **`CD020`** file's evaluation metrics, not the CD710.
